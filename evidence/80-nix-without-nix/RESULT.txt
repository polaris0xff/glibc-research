Q1 can a nixpkgs package be resolved and fetched with no nix?
   YES for anything the channel built. Three HTTPS endpoints:
   store-paths.xz (an index, and its redirect is a pin), <hash>.narinfo
   (signed metadata), and the NAR. Signature and NarHash both checked.
   NO for anything it did not: there is no evaluator here.

Q2 what does a fetched nixpkgs binary do?
   PT_INTERP = /nix/store/n51dhmdbik1kfrsm62j5knavmigwrl1a-glibc-2.42-84/lib/ld-linux-x86-64.so.2
   It is bound to an absolute /nix/store path and runs only where that
   store exists, or when handed its own loader: yes
   ⛔ That is why every nix bundler ends up shipping a store.
