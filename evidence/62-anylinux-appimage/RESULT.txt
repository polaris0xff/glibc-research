        ⛔ THE HONEST READING, IF THE ROWS COME OUT AS THEY DID FIRST TIME.
          Both deliver glibc everywhere and both keep glibc's throughput on a
          musl host. They are not distinguished by portability or by speed.
          What separates them is SHAPE: pgb hands you one ordinary ELF with
          no interpreter that writes nothing and mounts nothing; the AppImage
          hands you a runtime that mounts or extracts and then runs a shell.
          ⭐ And the AppImage stack reaches a class pgb does not: it bundles
          arbitrary shared libraries, so it packages software that cannot be
          statically linked at all -- which is precisely the criticism its
          own HOW-TO makes of the static approach.
