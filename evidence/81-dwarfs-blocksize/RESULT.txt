WHY THIS KNOB AND NOT THE RUNTIME'S OWN

  inside it, so it needs no patching at all.

  REUSE_CHECK_DELAY -- were not shippable because they are read from
  the environment. Wrong: they are compile-time constants laid out as
  patchable ASCII and `strings -a` finds them in this artefact.
