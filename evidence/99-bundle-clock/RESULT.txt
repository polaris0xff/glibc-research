WHY IT MATTERS
  uruntime keys its mountpoint on the artefact's CONTENT and leaves it
  alive for a few seconds after the process exits. A byte-identical
  copy therefore reuses it. experiments/90-'s cold column obtained
  "cold" by copying the file, and so reported a warm number whenever
  anything had run the same content within the window -- which is why
  docs/history/corrections.md C23 saw warm above cold in two of four
  runs and a 20x spread in the absolute figure.
